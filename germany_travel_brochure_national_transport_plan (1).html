App & Tracking

The DB Navigator App

Essential for every traveler. Shows real-time train coach formation (Wagenreihung) so you know exactly which letter section (A–G) on the platform your reserved carriage will halt.

Onboard Etiquette

Quiet Zones (Ruhebereich)

ICE trains feature dedicated quiet coaches where phone calls, loud music, and noisy chatter are strictly prohibited.

Local Transit

Ticket Validation (Entwerter)

Single paper metro and bus tickets must be stamped in the little red or yellow stamping box before boarding!

Luggage Storage

Station Lockers (Schließfächer)

Almost every Hauptbahnhof features automatic luggage lockers costing ~€3–€6 for 24 hours during layovers.

🏰 7 Iconic Destination Regions

  • Bavaria: Home to fairy-tale royal castles, Munich’s historic city center, and Germany’s highest alpine peak.
  • Berlin: Where iconic landmarks like the Brandenburg Gate meet UNESCO Museum Island and modern art.
  • Black Forest: Famous for its scenic waterfalls, relaxing thermal spas, and dense evergreen trails.
  • Rhine Gorge: A UNESCO valley lined with medieval clifftop castles and steep hillside vineyards.
  • Hamburg: A major maritime hub featuring historic red-brick warehouse canals and a world-famous modern concert hall.
  • Saxony & Dresden: Known for its grand Baroque palaces and dramatic rock-formation bridges.
  • Romantic Road: A famous scenic route connecting historic palaces to perfectly preserved medieval walled towns.

🚆 Transport Plan Summary

  • Deutschlandticket (€63/mo): Unlimited regional trains (RE/RB), S-Bahn, U-Bahn, and buses.
  • ICE High-Speed Network: Speeds up to 300 km/h connecting all major hubs.
  • Scenic Rails: Schwarzwaldbahn and Middle Rhine lines fully covered.
  • DB Navigator App: Real-time platform tracks (Gleis) and carriage layouts.
